# Prompt: storyboardy explainera DUO „Mgła” (9:16)

Do wklejenia w projekcie z animacjami. Narracja i koncept: `duo_explainer_mapa-relacji.txt`.

```
Work inside the existing project. First inspect its structure, tools, rendering setup, and visual assets. Preserve the current project and reuse its existing conventions where possible.

I want to create a short vertical (9:16) animated brand-awareness explainer for DUO® (Polish marriage and couples therapy, duo.pl) about this idea:
"Najpierw mapa. Potem droga." – why DUO starts with Mapa Relacji instead of starting with therapy or with a decision.

The core perspective shift: people in a relationship crisis live "in fog" – they feel something is wrong but cannot see what, so every decision (stay / leave / wait) looks wrong. DUO first makes the situation visible (questions → a conversation with a psychologist → a written picture of the relationship), and only then the person chooses one of three equal paths: rebuilding, time to decide, or parting with respect.

Keep it humane and balanced: never promise that the relationship will be saved, never show one path as the "right" one, never use the word "diagnoza". Mapa Relacji is not a diagnosis and not therapy.

THE LOGO IS THE STORY
The DUO logo literally shows two people sitting at a small table (terracotta figure on the left, green figure on the right, a sand-coloured table/bowl between them), and these three shapes also read as the word DUO. Use this as the spine of the film:
- Opening: the same two figures exist in the frame but apart (different rooms / different edges of the frame); the table between them is empty or missing.
- Middle: fog separates them; the table is the only thing that slowly becomes visible; the Mapa Relacji is unfolded ON the table.
- Ending: figures come to the table – not embracing, just sitting down to talk – the camera settles, the three shapes lock into the exact logo geometry and the wordmark DUO® appears. This must work for both readings: two partners at the table, or one person and a psychologist at the table. The ending shows clarity and a conversation, not reconciliation.
Use the real logo asset for the final lock-up; do not redraw it from memory. Design every style so that its characters are built from the logo's shapes from the first frame, so the final reveal feels inevitable.

First phase: storyboards only. Do not produce the finished animation yet.

If I provide a voiceover file, use it as the timing reference. Otherwise use the narration below (about 55 s) unchanged. Use the same narration and the same sequence of events in all storyboards so I can compare the visual styles fairly.

Create separate storyboard concepts for the exact same narration:

1. DUO paper cutout – layered paper shapes with soft inner shadows (the brand's infographic look), rounded mascot-like figures with no outlines; the fog is sheets of translucent tracing paper that are lifted away one by one.
2. Kinetic motion graphics – typography, lines and the three logo shapes carry the story; the hook question is the first frame; "ZOSTAĆ? ODEJŚĆ? CZEKAĆ?" dissolve into fog; no characters other than the logo shapes.
3. Blue-pen notebook – everything is drawn live with a blue ballpoint pen on warm lined paper (the handwritten style used in DUO worksheets); the fog is graphite smudge that gets erased; the map is literally drawn line by line.
4. Flat 2D figures in a 3D paper model of a flat – a dollhouse cross-section with volumetric fog between rooms; one controlled camera move that ends directly above the table, where the top view becomes the map and then the logo.
5. Minimalist visual metaphors – only three shapes on an empty background (two figures and the table); distance, fog, the table and three lines of paths tell everything.
6. Duotone risograph – two inks only, terracotta and green, with paper grain and slight misregistration; where the two inks overlap a third colour appears. Two inks = two people = DUO.
7. Contour-line map – the relationship as terrain drawn with cartographic contour lines; fog lies in the valleys and lifts; three paths appear on the terrain; the final contour lines tighten into the logo.
8. Fogged window – the whole film is seen through a steamed-up window pane; a hand wipes the glass a little more with each beat, revealing the scene behind; the last wipe reveals the two figures at the table.

For each style, prepare a storyboard of 7–9 frames covering the same narrative beats:
hook → everyday life side by side → fog and the three impossible decisions → "first see what is going on" (doctor analogy: examination before treatment, one second, no white coats) → Mapa Relacji in three steps (questions, conversation, written picture) → fog lifts, three equal paths → "we will not choose for you / happiness takes work" → "Najpierw mapa. Potem droga." → logo lock-up.

For every frame, specify:
* what is visible;
* what the camera or elements do;
* the voiceover line or phrase aligned to the frame;
* any on-screen text (Polish, correct diacritics, en dash "–", quotes „…");
* the transition to the next frame;
* the colour palette and emotional tone;
* where the two figures and the table are in the frame (so the logo reveal can be tracked across the film).

Make the storyboards easy to compare. If the project supports preview images, create a separate contact sheet for each style. Storyboard-level visuals only.

Brand and tone
Use the existing DUO logo and assets if they are present in the project. Colours:
#FBF9F5 ivory (background) · #F3EDE4 linen · #36493D forest green · #A9512F terracotta · #E07F78 rose · #97AC9C sage · #D9C7AE sand
Path colours: rose = rebuilding, terracotta = time to decide, green = parting with respect. Typography: an elegant serif for headlines, a clean sans for small text.
Tone: warm, calm, credible, grown-up. Avoid: therapy-room clichés (couch, notebook, tissue box), couples holding hands or hugging at the end, crying faces, stock-photo happiness, medical imagery beyond one second of the analogy, fear-based messaging, sales language, prices.

Stop point
After presenting all storyboards, stop and ask me to choose one style. Do not generate the voiceover, build the final animation, or render an MP4 until I select a storyboard.

NARRACJA
```
